    What Is FRC?

    FIRST® Robotics Competition (FRC) is a high school robotics league run by FIRST, a nonprofit. Every January, FIRST reveals a brand-new game. Teams design, build, and program a robot to play it, then compete at events in the spring.

    1. Early January

      Kickoff

      FIRST reveals the season’s new game.

    2. January to February

      Build Season

      Teams design, build, and program their robot.

    3. March and April

      Regionals

      Teams compete at events like the Pikes Peak and Hawaii Regionals.

    4. Spring

      Championship

      Top teams advance to the FIRST Championship.
